Our Vision

We are a team of A/V geeks with an absolute obsession for low-level engineering. We know firsthand how bloated, complex, and sometimes "anti-human" traditional professional broadcasting software can be. That is why LumenFrame was born with a single, pure mission:

To completely flatten the steep learning curve by hiding broadcast-grade, hardcore capabilities behind a "dummy-proof" exterior.

We firmly believe that great productivity software should be elegant, secure, and rock-solid, but its interaction should also be intuitive and perhaps a little bit witty. Work is hard enough—you shouldn't have to read a 500-page manual just to slice a video or set up a live broadcast. Trust your instincts, and let our underlying code do the heavy lifting.
