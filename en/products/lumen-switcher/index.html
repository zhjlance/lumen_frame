Lumen Switcher Official User Manual

Welcome to Lumen Switcher. Built on a native C++ core engine, this desktop-grade intelligent video slicing and rough-cut tool is designed to help you skip the tedious task of sorting through footage, generating ready-to-use timelines that seamlessly integrate with professional NLEs.

1. Introduction

Lumen Switcher abandons the bloated architecture of traditional editing software in favor of a minimalist workflow: Drag & Drop → Set Thresholds → Smart Scan → Export Project. Whether dealing with hours of live stream replays or massive conference recordings, it rapidly eliminates dead air and static scenes.

2. Media Import & Compatibility

  • Formats: Fully compatible with mainstream media containers, including .mp4, .mov, .mkv, .avi, and broadcast-grade .ts.
  • Underlying Decoder: Built-in FFmpeg and OpenCV hardware acceleration support ensures smooth parsing of H.264, HEVC (H.265), and ProRes.
  • Operation: Simply drag and drop files into the main interface. The engine will automatically extract foundational A/V properties and resolution info.

3. Dual-Modal Analysis Engine

Upon clicking "Render & Analyze", Lumen Switcher initiates a dual-track scanning mechanism:

  • Silence Gate: Automatically detects audio track levels. Continuous segments below the set threshold (default -35.0 dB) are marked as invalid.
  • Motion Gate: Compares frame-by-frame visual differences to eliminate prolonged static scenes.
  • Interactive Waveform: After analysis, a high-precision audio waveform is generated. You can double-click any segment to manually toggle its state.

4. Advanced Export & Workflows

We provide two distinct delivery paths to meet varying precision requirements:

  1. Export FCPXML (Highly Recommended): Instantly generate a Final Cut Pro XML file. You can import this directly into DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, or Premiere Pro. All cut points are perfectly preserved as a non-destructive timeline.
  2. Export Physical MP4 File:
    • Fast Mode (Remux): Cuts and seamlessly concatenates the video stream at keyframes without re-encoding. Extremely fast.
    • Precise Mode (Re-encode): Decodes and re-encodes frame-by-frame, ensuring absolute frame-accurate precision.

5. 30-Day Free Trial & Device Licensing

Lumen Switcher offers a generous evaluation period and utilizes industry-standard "Node-Locked" hardware binding:

  • 30-Day Unrestricted Trial: After initial installation, you can experience all export features free for 30 days. Once the trial expires, a permanent license must be purchased to continue exporting.
  • Activation: Enter your purchased License Key in the settings interface. The system captures a unique motherboard fingerprint to authorize the device. OS reinstallation does not affect license validity.
  • Unbinding/Transfer: While activated, you can release the current device's quota by deactivating the license, allowing you to activate the software on a new machine.
